Your music, without distractions.

Taki is a focused Android music player for personal music libraries. Listen online or keep music available offline without bringing server administration into the listening experience.

Taki works with Navidrome and other servers compatible with the Subsonic and OpenSubsonic APIs.

Features:
* Stream your personal music collection
* Download music for offline playback
* Connect to multiple compatible servers
* Browse playlists, albums, artists, songs, genres, and liked songs
* Search across your library
* Dark, distraction-free interface
* Android Auto and media-session integration

Taki is independently maintained as a personal open-source project, not a commercial service or company-backed product. It is based on Ultrasonic and distributed under the GNU GPLv3 license.

Source code and issues: https://github.com/churipakinti/taki-android
